# Assumptions and deviations

- This `test` branch is an averaged 20 V to 40 V CCM boost-converter study. It is not the original paper's 20 V to 350 V transformer/ZCS topology.
- Common controller input scales are `error_scale = 40 V` and `derivative_scale = 200 V/s`; both inputs are clamped to `[-1, 1]` before fuzzy/ANFIS evaluation.
- All unified comparison-model duty saturations were corrected to `[0.05, 0.95]`.
- Training data are generated by the same averaged plant used for MATLAB evaluation, with 24 varied scenarios and complete run-level train/validation/test separation.
- ANFIS is trained as a direct duty-target predictor on this branch. The deployed checkpoint is the minimum validation RMSE observed in the 40-epoch run; the actual minimum was epoch 9, not a forced epoch 22.
- The training labels are generated by the branch's existing teacher/controller definition. They are not claimed to be an ideal physical optimum.
- The final authoritative Simulink comparison uses the unified comparison `.slx` model and 300 ms simulation time. MATLAB averaged-backend metrics are reported separately.
- The unified Simulink model still shows ANFIS overshoot and final-value error after the scaling/limit correction. This is recorded as an open issue, not tuned away.
- Python execution was not used because `pandas` is unavailable in the environment.
